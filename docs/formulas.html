Offer Engine Formulas

Every formula the engine and the simulator use, with the file that holds it. All chances are for buying within 7 days, and \(\operatorname{clamp}(x)\) limits \(x\) to the range 0 to 1.

SimulatorKnows each Customer's hidden Ground Truth and uses it to decide who buys.
EngineSees only what a real marketplace would: past Offers and orders. Works from Estimated Lift.

The two sides never share numbers (ADR 0002). Comparing the engine's Estimated Lift with the simulator's true Lift shows how good the engine's guesses are.

Part 1 · Hidden from the engine

The simulator

Base chance: buying with no Offer sim/ground_truth.py › organic_weekly_chance

\[ \text{per session} = \sum_{c\,\in\,\text{categories}} \text{share}_c \times \text{cart}_c \times \text{order}_c \] \[ \text{sessions} = 7 \times \text{visit rate} \times \text{lapse factor} \times \text{demand} \] \[ \text{base} = 1 - e^{-\,\text{sessions}\, \times\, \text{per session}} \]

Sessions arrive at random (Poisson), so the chance of at least one order in 7 days is one minus \(e\) to the minus expected orders.

TermValue
\(\text{cart}_c\)\(\min(0.95,\ \text{CART\_RATE}_c \times \text{buy propensity} \times \text{demand})\)
\(\text{order}_c\)Order chance given a cart: 0.14 (electronics) up to 0.30 (grocery)
lapse factor0.03 if the Customer has lapsed, else 1
demand\(\sqrt{\text{season(day)}}\): weekends, the festive window and the peak sale week. The root puts half the effect on visits and half on conversion

Ground Truth is drawn at normal demand. When an Offer is sent, the simulator recomputes the base for that day's season and lapse state.

Ground Truth chance with an Offer sim/ground_truth.py › p_buy

\[ p_{\text{buy}}(\text{depth}) = \operatorname{clamp}\!\Big(\text{base} + \text{max\_lift} \times \big(1 - e^{-\text{depth}/k}\big)\Big) \]

The Offer adds up to max_lift to the base chance. Deeper Offers add more, with diminishing returns: at depth \(= k\) the Offer gives about 63% of max_lift, and at \(3k\) about 95%. Each Customer has one hidden formula per Offer Type.

max_lift is drawn once per Customer from their traits, times lognormal noise (mean 1, σ = 0.5):

Offer Typemean max_lift
% off, flat off\(0.13 \times \text{coupon affinity}^2\)
points multiplier\(0.4 \times 0.13 \times \text{coupon affinity}^2\)

So Customers who like coupons respond to discounts, and small-basket Customers in smaller cities respond to free shipping.

k is the saturation depth: 0.12 for % off and flat off, 0.5 for free shipping and Nudge, 1.0 for points. Each Customer's k varies around it (lognormal, σ = 0.2).

Sleeping Dogs are 3% of Customers. Any Offer makes them less likely to buy:

\[ \text{max\_lift} = -\,\text{base} \times U(0.3,\ 0.8) \quad \text{for every Offer Type} \]

If the base changes later (season, lapse), with_base scales this drop in proportion.

True Lift sim/offline.py › true_lift

\[ \text{true lift} = p_{\text{buy}}(\text{depth}) - \text{base} \;=\; \text{max\_lift} \times \big(1 - e^{-\text{depth}/k}\big) \]

Both terms use the send day's base. The second form holds unless the clamp kicks in. The Results page shows it as "Ground Truth Lift", and the live view shows it under "What the simulator knows".

Who actually buys sim/world/outcomes.py · customer_sim/roleplay.py

Every outcome ends in a weighted coin flip. With \(p = p_{\text{buy}}(\text{depth})\), the chance depends on the kind of Customer:

CustomerChance of buying
without a chat\(p\)
Tester who claimed\(\dfrac{p}{p + 0.25\,(1 - p)}\). A quarter of non-buyers also claim, so a Claim makes buying more likely
Customer Bot\(\operatorname{clamp}\big(p \times (1 + 0.3 \times \text{fit})\big)\), where the agent scores message fit from −1 to 1 (ADR 0004)

Message fit moves a Customer Bot's chance by at most ±30% of itself. Ground Truth still sets the base.

Part 2 · What the decision pipeline uses

The engine

Estimated Lift lift/segment_lift.py › fit

Learned from the history's random past Offers by offer-engine lift fit, per segment and Offer Type. A segment is loyalty tier × days since the last order (≤30, 31–90, more than 90 or never) × coupon user (redeemed at least 2 past Offers).

\[ \text{observed lift} = \frac{\text{buyers after a past Offer}}{\text{past Offers}} \;-\; \frac{\text{buyers after a no-offer moment}}{\text{no-offer moments}} \]

A no-offer moment is a time an Offer could have gone out but didn't: an abandoned cart, 45 days without an order, or the weekly Friday 18:00 broadcast slot. Moments inside a past Offer's 7 days don't count, and Holdout Customers are left out.

Past Offers had random depths, so the observed Lift is scaled up to the Lift at full depth:

\[ \text{saturation}_i = 1 - e^{-\text{depth}_i / k_{\text{prior}}} \qquad \text{estimated lift} = \frac{\text{observed lift}}{\operatorname{mean}_i(\text{saturation}_i)} \]

A segment with fewer than 30 past Offers of a type uses that type's average over all segments.

Lift at a given depth choosers/priors.py › lift_at

\[ \text{lift}(\text{depth}) = \text{estimated lift} \times \big(1 - e^{-\text{depth}/k_{\text{prior}}}\big) \]

The same curve shape as Ground Truth, with guessed k values instead of Ground Truth's. Until lift fit runs, a fixed prior replaces the estimated lift. The engine assumes a base chance of 0.10 for everyone.

The basket is the category's average basket, or the catalog average when the Offer has no category. A Customer earns 0.1 points per ₹.

Valuation choosers/rules_chooser.py › assess

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{lift} &= \text{lift}(\text{depth}) \\ \text{redeem chance} &= \operatorname{clamp}(0.10 + \text{lift}) \\ \text{value} &= \text{lift} \times \text{expected margin} \;-\; \text{redeem chance} \times \text{cost if redeemed} \end{aligned} \]

The expected margin is average basket × margin % for the Offer's category, else the Customer's focus category, else the catalog average.

For each option in the Eligible Set, the engine tries a grid of terms and keeps the ones with the highest value. The rules chooser sends the best option if its value is above 0, else No Offer. The agent sees the same Eligible Set and may choose differently, within the Rules.

The value charges the full expected cost but credits only the margin the Offer causes. An Offer to a Sure Thing has low Lift and a high chance of redemption, so its value comes out negative.

Weekly budget budget.py › budget_state · rules.py › eligible_set

\[ \text{remaining} = \max(\text{weekly budget} - \text{spent} - \text{reserved},\ 0) \]
  • spent is what this week's Redemptions actually cost.
  • reserved is the cost of every outstanding, unexpired Offer: worst-case cost by default, or expected cost in expected-cost mode.

The smaller of remaining and the per-Offer cost cap sets the most an option's terms may cost, such as the highest cap on a % off. An option leaves the Eligible Set when even its smallest terms cost more (for example a ₹50 cap).

Holdout rules.py › in_holdout

\[ \text{in Holdout} \iff \operatorname{int}\big(\text{SHA-256}(\text{Customer ID})_{[0:8]}\big) \bmod 10{,}000 \;\lt\; \text{holdout\_pct} \times 100 \]

A hash makes the Holdout permanent and the same on every run. A Tester copy shares its source Customer's Holdout.

Part 3 · Offline Policy runs

Scoring the results

Margin per Customer sim/offline.py

\[ \text{margin} = \sum_{\text{orders}} \text{amount} \times \text{margin\%} \;-\; \sum_{\text{returns}} \text{amount} \times \text{margin\%} \;-\; \sum_{\text{redeemed Offers}} \text{cost} \]

Incremental Margin sim/metrics.py › incremental_margin

\[ \text{Incremental Margin} = \sum_{\text{Customers}} \big(\text{margin}_{\text{Policy}} - \text{margin}_{\text{No Offers}}\big) \] \[ \text{per ₹ of spend} = \frac{\text{Incremental Margin}}{\text{total cost of redeemed Offers}} \]

Both runs share the same Customers, start time, days and seed, so the only difference is the Offers. This is the headline number the engine optimises.

Lift by decile sim/metrics.py › lift_deciles

Sent Offers are sorted by Estimated Lift and split into 10 equal groups. Each group shows its mean Estimated Lift next to its mean true Lift. If the engine's estimates are good, true Lift rises from decile 1 to decile 10.
