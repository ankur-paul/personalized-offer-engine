# Friction log

Entry format:

```
- [YYYY-MM-DD] **<skill / file / practice>** (bug | gap | improvement | disagreement): what happened, while doing what. Why it matters. Suggested fix.
```

Done as built: hour [t, t+1h) is processed as of its end, and the clock moves before the hooks. - [2026-10-23] **tickets/README.md › Rules for agents, rule 2** (gap): the rule lets an agent claim a ticket and "work on that branch" in place, so a parallel session checked out `t20-daily-sweep` in the primary worktree. - [2026-10-23] **docs/research/ecommerce-benchmarks.md, tickets/T15-calibration-check.md** (gap): the calibration targets were set before the generator's mechanics, and three can't hold together with them. Redemption per send (target 4) and cart recovery (3) assume most messages go unread, but every past Offer is read and every buyer in its window redeems. Lapse (6) and points never redeemed (11) assume we see each Customer's whole life, but 55% of Customers signed up before the 12-month history. Found while building T15. Each miss looked like a tuning job until the first measurement. Fix: when a ticket sets a numeric target, write down the mechanism that produces it, and check targets against the data window before tuning starts.
- [2026-10-23] **tickets/T16-estimated-lift-v1.md** (bug): T16 defined Lift as the purchase-rate difference at the past Offers' random depths, but T09's rules chooser and T11's agent prompt read `estimated_lift` as max Lift at full depth (`priors.lift_at`). Taken literally, the chooser would shrink every estimate a second time along the curve. Found while wiring the estimate into the pipeline. A field one ticket writes and an earlier ticket reads needs its unit stated in one place. Fix: give every shared field's unit in its `domain.py` comment, and have tickets cite that comment instead of restating the meaning.
- [2026-10-23] **test suite, /tmp** (gap): a full `uv run pytest` run, started while a 10k world sat in the scratchpad, failed with 63 failures and 164 errors (`sqlite3.OperationalError: disk I/O error`, `OSError` in `test_setup_wizard.py`). /tmp is a 7.5G tmpfs, and pytest keeps its last 3 runs' temp dirs there (1.9G, each with copies of the 50- and 2,000-Customer worlds).
